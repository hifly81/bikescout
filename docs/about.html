What is BikeScout?

BikeScout isfor riders who got tired of the "black box" nature of modern cycling apps.

We started this project in April 2026 to bring transparency to trail data. The goal was simple: intelligent data for raw riders.

Philosophy

We believe that planning a mission should be as technical as the ride itself. BikeScout is designed for who care about soil density, gradient analysis, and real-time weather vectors.

By open-sourcing the AI engine, We are inviting other developers and explorers to contribute to a standard where trail intelligence is accessible, customizable, and above all, truthful.
