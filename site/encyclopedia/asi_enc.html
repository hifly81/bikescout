ASI
Setup Intelligence

Core Concept: Mechanical Optimization

Actionable Setup Intelligence (ASI) is the BikeScout framework that bridges the gap between raw environmental data and mechanical execution. It eliminates "pressure guesswork" by calculating the optimal tire pressure (PSI/Bar) based on a multi-factor logic of rider weight, surface type, and moisture levels.

In tactical cycling, the interface between the tire and the ground is the most critical variable for mission success. ASI ensures this interface is optimized for either Efficiency, Compliance, or Flotation.

The Baseline: Volume and Load Distribution

ASI now starts from total system mass (rider + bike) and scales a discipline reference pressure non-linearly. Tire air volume is approximated from the square of the tire width, then adjusted for rim internal width, casing stiffness and tubeless/tube setup. Road and gravel split the load 45/55 front-to-rear; MTB, e-MTB and enduro use 40/60 to account for steep descents and rear-wheel bias.

Support_PSI = Base_PSI × (System_Mass / Reference_Mass)^1.25 × (Reference_Width / Tire_Width)^2
× (Reference_Rim_IU / Rim_IU)^0.5 × Casing_Factor × Tube_Factor
Front_PSI = Support_PSI × Grip_Factor × (Front_Share / 0.5)^0.6
Rear_PSI = Support_PSI × Grip_Factor × (Rear_Share / 0.5)^0.6

Tubeless setups keep the nominal pressure; inner tubes add roughly 18%. Stiffer casings (Trail, Enduro, DH) need progressively less pressure for the same support. A rim width outside the recommended range for the tire is flagged as a Rim-Width Mismatch warning.

The Tactical Strategy Logic

ASI doesn't just calculate numbers; it selects a Strategy. Depending on the data ingested from the TAEL® Model, Surface Analyzer and terrain engine, it applies an objective function balancing grip against rim-impact risk:

  • Mud Flotation: Triggered when the normalized Mud Index > 0.6. Pressure drops up to 15% to enlarge the contact patch.
  • Compliance: Triggered on technical terrain (rocks, roots). Pressure drops for casing deflection and grip.
  • Pinch-Flat Protection: On rocky S3+ terrain or high ascent, a support floor is enforced and a Pinch-Flat Warning is raised if grip optimization would go too low.
  • Efficiency: Triggered on smooth or paved surfaces. Pressure rises slightly to minimize rolling resistance.

Cross-System Integration

ASI is not an isolated tool. It is the final output of the BikeScout intelligence chain. It combines TAEL® soil saturation, surface breakdown and technical grade to produce equipment advice (inserts, sealant check, high-volume casing) and tread choice (Mud Spikes vs Semi-Slick). It also warns when current equipment (e.g., road tires on a 40% mud route, or a 2.5" tire on a 19mm rim) is a safety risk.

Mechanical Briefing Output

The result is a structured recommendation with machine-readable psi_front, psi_rear, advice and warnings, plus a standardized Tactical Display String:
29" wheels | 58mm | F: 19.9 PSI | R: 25.4 PSI (1.37/1.75 Bar) [Standard Setup].
