Metabolic & Thermal Modeling

Hydration Scout is BikeScout’s bio-energetic planning engine. It estimates fluids, carbohydrates and sodium for a ride by combining duration, intensity, rider mass, temperature and sweat-profile inputs. It is a planning model, not a clinical prescription.

Note: When no reliable weather temperature is available, BikeScout can use a conservative 20°C assumption for nutrition calculation only. The output marks this as an assumption and does not fabricate weather observations.

How the Model Works

The nutrition engine uses deterministic heuristics bounded by published ranges. Fluid targets start from rider mass and are adjusted by temperature and intensity. If the rider provides a measured sweat rate, that measurement replaces the population estimate.

// PLANNING INPUTS
duration_hours, temp_c, intensity_score, weight_kg, gender, sweat_profile, optional sweat_rate_ml_per_hour

Carbohydrate Tiers

Carbohydrate targets follow published endurance ranges and scale with intensity and duration. BikeScout reports the selected tier and ratio in the nutrition briefing:

  • Endurance / Recovery: lower target, typically around 40 g/h.
  • Tempo: medium target, typically around 60 g/h.
  • Race / Threshold: high target, typically 90 g/h and up to 120 g/h in long high-intensity cases, using multiple transportable carbs.

Safety, Assumptions and Evidence

BikeScout adds tactical alerts for heat stress, bonk risk, high sodium loss, over-hydration risk and uncertain inputs. The response includes data_basis, estimate_confidence, uncertainty_note, inputs_used and references so clients can distinguish measured inputs from estimates.
